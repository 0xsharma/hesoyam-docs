Privacy Policy for hesoyam

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This policy applies to the Android app hesoyam (package name com.hesoyam).

1. Advertising (AdMob / Google)

The free version of hesoyam shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC), using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. We do not sell your data. Ads are how the free app is funded.

Google’s own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data when you use our partners’ sites or apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Ad technology partners that may receive data for ad serving: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903

2. Removing ads

A one-time Google Play purchase removes ads forever on the Google account that is signed into Play Store on that device. After purchase, hesoyam does not request ads for that account. The unlock restores on other devices that use the same Google account. It is not a subscription.

3. Data AdMob may collect

We do not receive this data ourselves. It is collected and shared by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, as disclosed by Google. The wording below is from Google’s Google Play data disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares IP address, user product interactions, diagnostic information, and device/account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Data By default, the Google Mobile Ads SDK…
IP address Collects device’s IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of a device.
User product interactions Collects user product interactions and interaction information, including app launch, taps, and video views.
Diagnostic information Collects information related to the performance of your app and the SDK, including app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage.
Device and Account identifiers Collects Android advertising (ad) ID, app set ID, and, if applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the device.

In Play Console language, that covers device identifiers, approximate location (from IP address), and ad usage (impressions, taps, and related interaction data).

All of the user data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID in the device’s Settings (typically Privacy → Ads).

In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland, hesoyam shows Google’s consent message (UMP) before personalized ads are requested.

4. Accounts

hesoyam does not run its own account system. There is no sign-up, no hesoyam login, no Google Sign-In in the app, and no Play Games sign-in. Purchases use the Google account already on the phone (the account signed into Google Play Store). We never see your payment card details; Google Play processes the purchase.

5. What hesoyam itself stores

Cheat sending uses Bluetooth to a PC you have already paired. That traffic stays between the phone and that PC. We do not operate a backend that receives cheats, game names, or Bluetooth identifiers.

Favourites and similar preferences are stored on the device only (and may be included in Android backup if you have backup enabled).

hesoyam does not collect your name, email, or precise GPS location, and does not request location permission. Bluetooth scan is declared neverForLocation.
