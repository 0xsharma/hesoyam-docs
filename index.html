Privacy Policy for hesoyam

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This policy applies to the Android app hesoyam (package name com.hesoyam), provided by the developer at shivam691999@gmail.com.

hesoyam is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

1. Advertising (AdMob / Google)

The free version of hesoyam shows ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC), using the Google Mobile Ads SDK: a banner on the game list, and a full-screen interstitial when you leave the cheat pad. We do not sell your data. Ads fund the free app.

Google and its ad partners may use advertising identifiers, IP address, and similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize ads (or to serve non-personalized ads if you decline personalization where that choice is offered).

Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data when you use partners’ sites or apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Ad technology partners that may receive data for ad serving: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903

2. Removing ads

A one-time Google Play purchase (not a subscription) removes ads on the Google account already signed into Play Store on the phone. After a completed purchase, hesoyam does not request ads for that account. The unlock restores on other devices that use the same Google account.

Ads stay off for as long as Google Play shows that purchase as owned. If Google refunds or revokes the purchase, ads return on the next successful Play check. A pending (unfinished) payment does not remove ads.

3. Data AdMob may collect

hesoyam does not run a server that receives this data. Google collects and shares it through the Mobile Ads SDK. In AdMob we may see aggregated reports (for example impressions and estimated revenue), not a profile of you as an individual. The wording below is from Google’s Google Play data disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares IP address, user product interactions, diagnostic information, and device/account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Data By default, the Google Mobile Ads SDK…
IP address Collects device’s IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of a device.
User product interactions Collects user product interactions and interaction information, including app launch, taps, and video views.
Diagnostic information Collects information related to the performance of your app and the SDK, including app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage.
Device and Account identifiers Collects Android advertising (ad) ID, app set ID, and, if applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the device.

That includes device identifiers, approximate location inferred from IP address (not GPS), and ad usage such as impressions, taps, and related interaction data. hesoyam does not request Android location permission and does not collect precise GPS location.

All of the user data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID in the device’s Settings (typically Privacy → Ads).

Where Google requires it — in particular the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland — hesoyam shows Google’s User Messaging Platform consent message before ads are requested. If you decline personalized ads, Google may still serve non-personalized or limited ads where its policies allow. That consent prompt can appear at launch even after you buy Remove ads, because consent is requested when the app opens; ads themselves are not requested after a completed purchase.

4. Accounts and purchases

hesoyam does not run its own account system. There is no sign-up, no hesoyam login, no Google Sign-In in the app, and no Play Games sign-in. Purchases use the Google account already on the phone. Google Play Billing processes the payment. We never see your payment card details. On the device we only cache a yes/no flag that ads were removed; Play overwrites that flag on each successful check.

5. What hesoyam itself stores

Cheat sending uses Bluetooth to a PC you pair in Android and Windows settings. That traffic stays between the phone and that PC. We do not operate a backend that receives cheats, game names, or Bluetooth identifiers.

On the device only, hesoyam may store: favourites, last selected game, keyboard timing and similar preferences, the ads-removed cache, and the Bluetooth address of the last PC you connected. If Android backup or a device-to-device transfer is enabled, that on-device data may be copied with the rest of the app.

Bluetooth scanning is declared neverForLocation: the app does not use Bluetooth to derive your location.
